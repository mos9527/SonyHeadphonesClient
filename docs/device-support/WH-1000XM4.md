## Notes

**Multipoint needs the alert handshake.** Writing `D8 D2 01 <v>` without first sending
`94 01 00` (ALERT_SET_STATUS, `FIXED_MESSAGE = 1`, `ENABLE = 0`) is silently dropped: the
device echoes the old value and a read-back does not change either. With the alert enabled
the device answers `99 01 <MT> 01` (`MT = 0x06` when switching on, `0x07` when switching off),
the link drops, and `98 01 <MT> 01` has to be sent to confirm. The new value only survives
after reconnecting. This is why multipoint looked "rejected by the device" until the alert
path was implemented - it is not a permission problem.

**Automatic power off is two-state on this firmware.** `F8 04 01 10 00` ("off when taken
off") and `F8 04 01 11 00` ("off") are accepted; the 5/30/60/180-minute codes seen on other
models are all normalised back to `10 00`. Offering the timer list in the UI would be
misleading for this device.

**Voice passthrough is sticky.** `focus` (`0x01` = on) stays set when switching back to noise
cancelling, and it is only meaningful in ambient mode. Resetting it requires going through
ambient mode first.

**Voice guidance is Table#2 only.** `48 01 01 <v>` with reply `49 01 01 <v>`; there is no
query command, so the current value cannot be read on connect.

**Not present on this device:** adaptive sound control is implemented in the app, not in the
protocol - the app only sends ordinary NC/AMB settings, so there is nothing for the client to
toggle. Head gesture, the WH-1000XM5 listening modes and "capture voice during a call" do not
exist on the XM4 either.

## Open items

- Volume and track controls were not exercised at the protocol level (they rely on AVRCP on the

- Wearing status, the assignable button and paired-device management were not exercised.

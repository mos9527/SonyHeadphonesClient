WH-1000XM3
---

Tested on firmware 4.5.2, MDR V1 (protocol `0x4010`), Linux (BlueZ). Packet capture:
[`tests/WH-1000XM3-4.5.2/`](../../tests/WH-1000XM3-4.5.2/) (initialization, then volume,
play/pause, NC/AMB, ambient level, an equalizer preset and DSEE changed from the client).
Bluetooth addresses - the headset's own and the host's - are replaced with placeholders.
The NC/AMB button on the headset, the touch panel switch, automatic power off and voice
guidance were verified in separate sessions.

**NOTE:** **✅**: Supported, ❌: Unsupported, **?**: Untested, **~**: Supported officially, pending implementation.

In this table **✅** means the setting was changed from the client and the device confirmed
it with a notification. **?** means the device advertises the function and answered during
initialization, but the write path was not exercised.

Notes:

- **The device answers a request before it acknowledges it.** Every `*_RET_*` reply in the
  captures arrives ahead of the ACK for the `*_GET_*` request it answers (notifications after
  a `*_SET_*` come after the ACK). Before this was handled, the reply to
  `CONNECT_GET_PROTOCOL_INFO` was lost while initialization still waited on the ACK, and the
  session timed out right after the handshake.
- **Adaptive Sound Control, Sound Position Control, Surround (VPT)**: advertised as
  `AUTO_NC_ASM`, `SOUND_POSITION` and `VPT`, but libmdr does not request them yet.
- **NC Optimizer, Bluetooth Connection Quality**: advertised as `NC_OPTIMIZER` and
  `CONNECTION_MODE` (the client lists "Connection mode" as available), but the client has no
  control for either.
- **General settings**: `GENERAL_SETTING2` is `TOUCH_PANEL_SETTING` (boolean); switching it
  is confirmed with `GENERAL_SETTING_NTFY_PARAM`. `GENERAL_SETTING1` is
  `ASSIGNABLE_KEY_SETTING`, a list (NC/AMB, Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa); the client only
  shows boolean general settings, so it is not offered.
- **Automatic power off** is written with `SYSTEM_SET_EXTENDED_PARAM`. The device
  acknowledges every value but sends no notification. V1 has no 15-minute element
  (`AutoPowerOffElementId` knows 5/30/60/180 minutes and off), so the client's
  "15 minutes" entry is rejected by libmdr and nothing is sent.
- **Voice guidance** on/off is confirmed with `VOICE_GUIDANCE_NTFY_PARAM`. The device has no
  voice guidance volume.
- **Changing the NC/AMB mode on the headset** arrives as `NCASM_NTFY_PARAM`, preceded by a
  `PLAY_NTFY_PARAM` with the current volume, and the client follows it. Volume, play/pause,
  Quick Attention and the NC Optimizer were not tried from the headset side.
- Requests for `INSTRUCTION_GUIDE` device info, `COMMON_GET_UPSCALING_EFFECT` and some
  `UPDT_GET_PARAM` inquiries are acknowledged and never answered. Initialization is not
  affected.
- Changing the equalizer preset made the device raise `ALERT_NTFY_PARAM`
  `DISCONNECT_CAUSED_BY_CONNECTION_MODE_CHANGE` with a yes/no choice. The client answered
  `NEGATIVE`, and the device notified the new preset (`EQEBB_NTFY_PARAM`) anyway, without
  disconnecting.
